# Ancient Near East: scope of the full read

What is read in full from the originals, from which edition, under which
terms, and what is left out and why. The list of texts with their line counts
and reading chunks is `texts.yaml`, generated by
`python3 tools/aneread.py scope --write` from the downloads in
`data/raw/ane/` (fetched by `python3 tools/anefetch.py`; not committed).

**In scope: 1,610 texts, 117,278 lines, 54 reading chunks** of about 2,000
lines (`python3 tools/aneread.py plan`).

| Tradition | Texts | Lines |

| Akkadian | 748 | 46,057 |
| Ugaritic | 55 | 4,071 |
| Egyptian | 27 | 8,979 |
| Hittite (and the Akkadian versions of Hittite treaties) | 365 | 21,555 |
| Northwest Semitic inscriptions | 28 | 818 |

A "line" is the edition's unit:
- a verse of a reconstructed text (eBL);
- a line of a composite text (ETCSL, ORACC);
- a tablet line (EUPT, ORACC tablets);
- a sentence (TLA);
- a clause or Kolon (hethiter.net);
- an inscription line.

## Out of scope, and why

**Not obtainable as machine-readable original text.** These were searched for
and not found; nothing is tagged from memory as if read.
- **Sargon Birth Legend** (CT 13 42–43). CDLI lists the tablet (P365306) with
  no transliteration, and no other open edition was found. The Sumerian
  Sargon legend (ETCSL 2.1.4) is read. The existing unit `akk-sargon-birth`
  stays as secondary knowledge.
- **Ugaritic:** Rephaim (KTU 1.20–1.22, empty placeholders on EUPT), 1.8,
  Marzeah (1.114), 1.108 and the other KTU 1.x ritual and religious texts.
  EUPT serves nothing for these, and no other open, lemmatized KTU source
  exists (DULAT is commercial). The KTU print on the Internet Archive is
  borrow-only and was not used as data.
- **Egyptian:** Coffin Texts (CT 75–80, 1130), the Leiden hymns to Amun
  (pLeiden I 350), the Memphite Theology, and Demotic (Setne). These are not
  in the 2018 TLA export. The live TLA is behind a bot gate, and the Hugging
  Face TLA sets are shuffled sentences without text ids.
- **Northwest Semitic:** Deir ʿAlla Combination II, Sefire III, Bar-Rakib,
  the Nerab stelae, and Aramaic Ahiqar (TAD C1.1). The CAL asks not to be
  scraped and offers dumps on request.
- **Akkadian:** eBL chapters whose reconstruction is not online, e.g. the
  Neo-Assyrian Atrahasis fragments, the *Aluzinnu* text, and parts of Erra.
  Mari prophecies (ARM 26) and other Old Babylonian letters are not in an
  open lemmatized edition.

**Left out by choice** (listed in `texts.yaml` under `left_out` where they
were downloaded):
- **Hittite CTH 389:** 72 prayer fragments, transliteration only, no
  translation.
- **Hittite CTH 68, 106, 409:** TLHdig manuscript transliterations only, with
  several overlapping manuscripts and no composite or translation.
  Muršili–Kupanta-Kurunta, Tudḫaliya–Kurunta (Bronze Tablet) and Tunnawiya
  were not read in that form; read them when the portal publishes editions.
- **eBL divination, medicine and lexical series** (Šumma ālu, Enūma Anu Enlil,
  Sakikkû, Tintir): omen and medical compendia whose entries do not form
  narratives or prayers. The literary, incantation and šuʾila genres are in.
- **ORACC SAA 8** (astrological reports), **SAA 10** (letters from scholars),
  **SAA 12** (grants and decrees) and **SAA 13** (letters from priests):
  downloaded but not read, being administrative and scholarly correspondence.
  The letters with religious content (the substitute king, prophecy) are a
  candidate for a later selective pass.
- **The rest of RINAP and RIBo:** the royal inscriptions that are mostly
  building and campaign accounts. Kept are the texts with religious
  narrative or a bearing on the Hebrew Bible, listed above.
- **ORACC AEMW/Ugarit legal and administrative texts** that do not meet the
  religious-vocabulary filter (about 1,760 texts).
- **ORACC epsd2/literary composites outside ETCSL's catalogue:** 2 texts.
- **Witnesses that repeat a main text** (Egyptian, Hittite and
  Old/Middle/Standard Babylonian versions) are **in** scope and read as
  separate units.

## Reading chunks

`texts.yaml` assigns every text to a chunk, in this order: ETCSL; ORACC
Sumerian composites; eBL; ORACC SAA, royal inscriptions, GKAB, Amarna,
Ugarit; EUPT; TLA; hethiter.net; Northwest Semitic. A text longer than 1.5
chunks would be split at its section boundaries (none is). Assignments are
fixed once reading starts. The protocol is `data/motif-tags/ane/README.md`.
