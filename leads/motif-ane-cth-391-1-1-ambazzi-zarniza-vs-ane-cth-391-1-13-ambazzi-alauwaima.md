    it is white, so may the gods clean the evil illness out of this man''s body (weight 7.1); White wool

    off and laid in a basket, pulling the evil out of every limb (weight 7.1); Tin wrapped in a bowstring
    is tied on the patient''s right hand and foot, then wound on a mouse, which is released to carry the
    evil over high mountains, deep valleys and far roads (weight 7.1); Wood and grain are roasted and
    the fire quenched with water: as I have quenched this, so may the evil be quenched over the patients
    (weight 7.1); The afflicting deity is told to stop fighting the patients and fight this bolt (a model
    door-bolt) instead, and drive the evil away with it (weight 7.1); Living goat bears the people''s
    sins away into the wilderness (weight 6.2). Pair score 55.9.'
