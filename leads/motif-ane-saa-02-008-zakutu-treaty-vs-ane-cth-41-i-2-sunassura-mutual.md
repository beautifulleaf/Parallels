    among courtiers, kin or the people, he must report it and seize and kill the plotters; A loyalty oath
    (adê) is imposed by the Assyrian king (or queen mother) on vassals or subjects, binding them to him
    and his heir ''from this day as long as we live''; The treaty is sworn before a list of gods (including
    the vassal''s own gods) or before the planets and stars, who guarantee it.'
