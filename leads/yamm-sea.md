title: 'al-yamm: the sea word reserved for Moses'
summary: The Quran's usual word for sea is bahr, but al-yamm appears 8 times across 4 surahs, always in
  the Moses/Pharaoh narrative. It is the Semitic sea word shared with Hebrew yam, Ugaritic Yam, and Mandaic
  yama, and borrowed into New Kingdom Egyptian.
